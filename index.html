    How is this different from hiring a freelancer?

    A freelancer is one person on their own. INTACT gives you a managed team: the right level for each kind of work, documented processes, quality checks and a senior HR partner to escalate to.

    Where is the team based?

    Our HR professionals work from our office in Karachi, Pakistan, on U.S. business hours. We also have an office in Dallas, Texas. You get the overlap you need at a fraction of U.S. cost.

    Do they know U.S. employment law?

    Yes. Every professional is trained on U.S. employment law and passes our U.S. HR assessment and an English test before joining a Pod. For legal questions, we work alongside your employment lawyer.

    How quickly can you start?

    Usually within [X] days of our first call. Hiring in-house takes a median of 39 days just to fill the role.

    How do you keep employee data confidential?

    Your HR professional signs a confidentiality agreement and works inside your systems, with access you grant and can remove at any time.

    What if my HR person is away or leaves?

    You're never relying on one person. Every Pod is managed, with documented processes, backup cover and senior HR escalation, so the work carries on.

    Can I change hours or stop?

    Yes. Pay month to month, and add or reduce hours and roles as your needs change. Commit to 6 or 12 months to save 10% or 15%. Changes apply from the next month.

    What does the 30-day money-back guarantee cover?

    If you're not satisfied with your first 30 days of service, we refund your first 30 days' payment, no questions asked. Full terms are in the service agreement.
